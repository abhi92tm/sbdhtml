1. Overview
This Delivery Policy outlines the terms governing the delivery of products and services offered by Shuruaat Bites & Delights, a Unit of Shuruaat Kitchen.

By placing an order with us, you agree to the terms set out in this Delivery Policy.


2. Delivery Coverage
We currently provide delivery services within our approved service areas in and around Pune, Maharashtra.

Delivery availability may vary depending on:

Delivery location

Distance from the preparation location

Order value

Delivery slot availability

Weather conditions

Operational capacity

We reserve the right to decline delivery requests outside our service areas.


3. Delivery Schedule
Delivery dates and times are estimates and are communicated during order confirmation.

Delivery schedules may vary based on:

Product type

Customization requirements

Order size

Corporate or event requirements

Traffic conditions

Weather conditions

Operational factors

While we make every effort to deliver on time, delivery times cannot be guaranteed.


4. Delivery Charges
Delivery charges, if applicable, will be communicated during order confirmation or included in the quotation or invoice.

Delivery charges may vary based on:

Delivery location

Distance

Order value

Urgent delivery requirements

Special handling requirements

Any applicable delivery charges must be paid along with the order payment unless otherwise agreed in writing.


5. Order Confirmation
A delivery order is considered confirmed only when:

The order has been accepted by us.

Required details have been provided by the customer.

Applicable payment has been received.

The delivery slot has been confirmed.

Online orders are confirmed only after successful payment confirmation.


6. Customer Responsibilities
Customers must provide accurate:

Recipient name

Mobile number

Delivery address

Landmark information

PIN code

Delivery instructions

Customers are responsible for ensuring that:

The delivery address is correct.

The recipient is available at the delivery location.

Necessary access permissions are available.

Security clearances are arranged where required.

Incorrect or incomplete information may result in delivery delays or failed deliveries.


7. Corporate and Office Deliveries
For corporate, office, and institutional deliveries, customers must ensure:

Security approvals are arranged in advance.

Entry permissions are available.

Contact persons are available to receive deliveries.

Any gate pass or visitor requirements are communicated beforehand.

Delays caused by office security procedures, restricted access, or recipient unavailability shall not be considered delivery failures on our part.


8. Delivery Attempts
Our delivery personnel or delivery partners may attempt to contact the recipient using the phone number provided during the order process.

If the recipient is unavailable, unreachable, or refuses delivery, the order may be classified as a failed delivery.

Additional delivery charges may apply for re-delivery requests.


9. Failed Deliveries
A delivery may be considered unsuccessful if:

The recipient is unavailable.

The provided contact number is unreachable.

The delivery address is incorrect.

Access is denied or unavailable.

The customer requests last-minute changes that cannot be accommodated.

For perishable products, re-delivery may not always be possible.

Refunds may not be available for failed deliveries caused by customer-related issues.


10. Delivery Completion and Risk Transfer
Delivery shall be deemed completed when the order is handed over to:

* The customer
* The recipient named in the order
* A family member
* An office receptionist
* A security desk
* An authorized representative

Responsibility for the product transfers to the customer upon successful delivery.
Customers should inspect deliveries upon receipt and report any issues promptly.


11. Pickup Orders
Customers may choose to collect orders directly from the designated pickup location, where available.

For pickup orders:
Customers must collect products within the agreed collection time.

Delayed collection may affect product quality.

We are not responsible for deterioration resulting from delayed pickup.


12. Delivery Delays
Delivery delays may occur due to circumstances including:

Traffic congestion

Weather conditions

Road closures

Vehicle breakdowns

Accidents

Public restrictions

Supplier delays

Force majeure events

Any circumstances beyond our reasonable control

In such situations, we will make reasonable efforts to inform customers about revised delivery schedules.


13. Product Condition Upon Delivery
Customers should inspect products immediately upon delivery.

If any of the following are observed, customers should notify us as soon as possible:

Significant transit damage

Incorrect item delivered

Missing items

Product quality concerns

Supporting photographs may be requested for verification.

Claims should be submitted in accordance with our Cancellation, Return and Refund Policy.


14. Food Safety After Delivery
Once delivery has been completed:

Customers are responsible for appropriate storage and handling.

Products should be consumed within a reasonable period.

Refrigeration requirements, where applicable, should be followed.

We are not responsible for product deterioration caused by improper storage or delayed consumption after delivery.


15. Special Delivery Requests
Customers may request:

Specific delivery time windows

Special handling instructions

Multiple delivery locations

Corporate delivery coordination

While we will make reasonable efforts to accommodate such requests, fulfillment cannot be guaranteed and additional charges may apply.


16. Delivery Partner Services
Where third-party delivery partners are engaged, delivery timelines may be affected by their operational constraints.

We will continue to provide reasonable support and communication to customers regarding delivery status.
