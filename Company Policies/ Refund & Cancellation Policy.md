1. Overview
At Shuruaat Bites & Delights, we strive to provide high-quality food, bakery products, customized cakes, snacks, meal boxes, catering services, and corporate food orders.

As most of our products are freshly prepared, customized, and perishable in nature, this Cancellation, Return and Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which cancellations, returns, replacements, and refunds may be accepted.

By placing an order, making a payment, or accepting a quotation, you agree to this Policy.


2. Cancellation Policy
2.1 Cancellation Requests
Customers may request cancellation within 1 hour of payment by contacting us through:

Email: admin.shuruaat@zohomail.in

Phone: +91 8660802809

Cancellation requests are subject to approval and order status.


2.2 Non-Cancellable Orders
Cancellation requests may not be accepted if:

Ingredient procurement has commenced.

Product preparation has commenced.

Baking or production has commenced.

Customization work has commenced.

Packaging has commenced.

Dispatch has commenced.

Catering preparation has commenced.

Event preparation has commenced.

Customized, made-to-order, and perishable food items become non-cancellable once preparation begins.


2.3 Corporate, Bulk and Event Orders
Corporate, catering, event, and bulk orders involve advance planning, ingredient procurement, logistics arrangements, and production scheduling.

Accordingly:

Advance payments may become non-refundable once procurement or production activities begin.

Quantity reductions after confirmation may be treated as partial cancellations.

Cancellation terms specified in an accepted quotation or purchase order shall take precedence.


2.4 Cancellation by Shuruaat Bites & Delights
We reserve the right to cancel any order in cases including but not limited to:

Product or ingredient unavailability

Operational constraints

Delivery restrictions

Payment failure

Incorrect customer information

Suspected fraud

Safety concerns

Circumstances beyond reasonable control

Where we cancel an order for reasons not attributable to the customer, any refundable amount paid will be refunded or credited at our discretion.

The following do not qualify for returns:

Customized products

Personalized cakes

Made-to-order products

Perishable food items

Products improperly stored after delivery

A return-related claim may be evaluated if the customer receives:

4. Replacement Policy
4.1 Replacement Eligibility
Replacement requests may be considered for:

Wrong product delivered

Significant delivery damage

Verified quality issues

Confirmed order fulfillment errors


4.2 Replacement Limitations
A replacement may not be possible when:

Products are customized or personalized

Products are perishable and a safe replacement cannot reasonably be arranged

The product has already been substantially consumed

The complaint is reported outside the claim period

Where replacement is not feasible, we may offer an alternative resolution including partial refund, full refund, store credit, or order correction.


5. Refund Policy
5.1 Eligible Refund Situations
Refunds may be considered for:

Incorrect products delivered

Significant order fulfillment errors

Duplicate payments

Services paid for but not provided

5.2 Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds will generally not be provided in cases involving:

Delays caused by incorrect delivery information

Delays caused by weather conditions

Delays caused by traffic conditions

Delays caused by force majeure events

Products consumed or partially consumed

Products improperly stored after delivery

Orders cancelled after preparation has commenced

Customized cakes, personalized bakery products, made-to-order products, and specially prepared food items are generally non-refundable once production has commenced.
